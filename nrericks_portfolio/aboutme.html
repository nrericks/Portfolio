Hello!
My name is Nicholas.

I was born and raised in Long Island, New York. Home for me was Brookhaven, which is just about an hours train ride away from New York City. After graduating from Bellport Highschool in 2014, I breifly pursued further education through the State University of New York (SUNY) system at the Suffolk County Community College.


After some time, I took a break from academics and eventually enlisted into the United States Navy. I was shipped out for basic training in September of 2016, and graduated shortly after in November. Afterwards, I was sent to Naval Base Corry Station in Pensacola, Florida. It's there that I attended my Navy 'A-School' which is where I would become qualified as a Cryptologic Technician in March of the following year.


After graduating A-school, I was shipped out to my first duty station of NIOC Hawaii, situated in Oahu, Hawaii. As I waited some time for my security access to be approved to do my designated job, my immediate duties upon arriving on station were diverse, but the most notable responsibility was giving historical tours of Pearl Harbor for both active and retired military members as well as their families.


Once my clearences were approved, I had finally started working in my designated field. A brief summary of my duties within the military intelligence community was to work along side a joint military team to satisfy specific information needs for the Department of Defense. We also would augment active military operations by providing indications and warnings to U.S. military platforms and their allies.


I honorably seperated from the United States Navy in September of 2022. I briefly returned home to Long Island to finish my associates degree in General Studies. Shortly after, I would move back to Hawaii to be reunited with the girl who is now my fiancee, as well as her daughter.


Deciding to pursue my education further towards a Bachelors Degree, I applied to the state of Hawaii's system. After being accepted by The University of Hawaii West Oahu campus, I've been working towards a Bachelor's in Creative Media since Janurary of 2023. I would also re-enlist in the U.S. Navy as an active reservist not too long after.


I am currently on track to finish my degree by the end of the Fall 2026 semester. My immediate plan is to find work that is both meaningful and allows to me to care for my family of four. It is my and my fiancee, as well as her teenage daughter and our new infant daughter. My immediate priority in life at the moment is to ensure their security, which is why I seek employment that allows me to do so.
